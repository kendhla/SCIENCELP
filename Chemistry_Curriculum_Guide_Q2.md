## PERFORMANCE STANDARDS AND SUGGESTED PERFORMANCE TASK

**By the end of the quarter, learners:**
- Apply research skills to discover how scientists have impacted our lives
- Recognize the significant role of different agencies in the Philippines (DOST, DTI, FDA, DENR) in scientific innovation and development
- Show critical thinking in explaining the different substances in household and personal care products, including their benefits and potential risks
- Demonstrate appropriate and safe handling and use of chemicals
- Explain that chemical reactions occur in human activities and the environment
- Recognize that several chemical solutions pose a threat to the community

**Suggested Performance Task:**
Look for household or personal care products and identify their active ingredients. Then, research each active ingredient by identifying its brief description, other uses, and potential impact on humans and the environment. Create a comprehensive report or digital presentation with recommendations for safe use and proper disposal.
