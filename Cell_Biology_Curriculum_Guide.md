## ASSESSMENT STRUCTURE

Each topic includes two types of assessments:

| Assessment Type | Items | Purpose | Scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Formative Assessment (FA)** | 10 items | Check for understanding, guide instruction, identify learning gaps | Throughout the topic |
| **Graded Competency Check (GCC)** | 15 items | Verify mastery of learning competencies, generate grades | End of topic |
| **Total Assessment Items per Topic** | **25 items** | Comprehensive coverage of all learning competencies | Combined formative and summative |

**Assessment Distribution:**
- Formative items are low-stakes, used for feedback and instructional adjustment
- GCC items are higher-stakes assessments counting toward grades
- Performance tasks (Diffusion and Photosynthesis) are embedded within respective topics
